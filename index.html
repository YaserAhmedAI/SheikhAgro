Who We Are

A Nursery Built on Science & Tradition

Sheikh Agro is a dedicated high density apple nursery rooted in the fertile valleys of Jammu & Kashmir. We combine traditional farming wisdom with modern orchard science to deliver certified HD apple plants suited to every climate in India.

High Density Planting (HDP) systems allow apple trees to be grown much closer together on dwarfing rootstocks — maximising land use, accelerating early fruiting, and reducing input costs per unit of land.

Our imported nursery stock is raised with precision, ensuring true-to-variety, disease-free plants ready to perform from day one in your orchard.
